Why spacing works

Memory fades fast after first exposure. Each successful recall slows the next round of forgetting, so reviews can move further apart.

Core claim. Check against the 2019 replication.
9 Nov

The forgetting curve

Ebbinghaus measured how much of a list he could relearn after a delay. Most loss happens in the first day; what survives is fairly stable.

So day-1 review matters most.
9 Nov

The practical rule: review just before you would forget. Too early wastes effort; too late means relearning from zero. A schedule is only a guess at that moment for each card.

Scheduling

Simple schedules double the gap after each correct answer. Better ones track how hard each item was and adjust per item.
